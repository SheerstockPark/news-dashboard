SHEERSTOCK PARK · What We Build Next
Proposed follow-on projects to the News Terminal — for review & selection

The News Terminal is live: the dashboard, 06:00 & 20:00 briefings, the Saturday Desk Review, and ~1-minute urgent alerts — all self-running, at zero monthly cost. The same approach (AI + automation on free infrastructure) can now be pointed at the next problem. Four candidates below; each is buildable in days, not months.

Option A — The Partner's Brief recommended first

One Monday-morning email that makes the week legible built for Josh

Josh isn't on the desk every day — this is the executive lens: everything that matters, once a week, in three minutes.

  • The regime in one paragraph — what kind of market this is right now, and what changed last week.
  • What the desk was pinged on — the urgent alerts that fired, and what followed.
  • Positioning check — where funds are crowded in crude/products (free CFTC data): "speculators most net-long since March" is a partner-grade signal.
  • Three sharp questions to ask the desk this week — AI-drafted from the data, e.g. "Funds are max-long while OPEC adds supply — what's our exposure if the crowd unwinds?" Walking in with the right questions is the partner's job; this arms it.
  • The week ahead — catalysts with dates.

Lands Monday 07:00 · builds on the Terminal's data + rails · ~2 evenings to build · £0/month

Option B — The Desk Journal

A trade log that remembers what the world looked like Neil + Josh oversight

Neil logs a position in 30 seconds — instrument, direction, thesis. The system automatically attaches what the Terminal knew at that moment: the headlines, the sentiment, the prices. Then every week, an AI review: which theses played out, which got blindsided, what the desk systematically over- or under-reacts to.

  • For Neil: a disciplined journal with zero admin burden.
  • For Josh: the partner view — open book, live theses, weekly review notes. Full visibility without looking over anyone's shoulder.

Private & access-controlled · compounds with the Terminal's archive · ~3 evenings · £0/month

Option C — Market & Counterparty Intelligence

A watchlist with a memory firm-wide

Name the companies that matter — counterparties, majors, competitors, key customers — and the Terminal's archive starts tracking them: news, results, credit signals, personnel moves. A weekly intel digest per name, and an alert if something big breaks on any of them.

Rides the existing 57-feed archive · ~2 evenings · £0/month

Option D — Scenario Playbooks

Decide the response before the shock preparedness

Pre-agreed one-page playbooks for the big five: Hormuz closure, OPEC surprise, SPR release, Gulf hurricane, rate shock — first moves, what to watch, historical analogues. AI-drafted, desk-edited once. When an urgent alert fires on one of these scenarios, the email links straight to the playbook.

Integrates with the live urgent alerts · ~1–2 evenings · £0/month

Suggested order

1. Partner's Brief (A) — fastest to ship, immediately useful to Josh, and it makes the whole Terminal's value visible at partner level every Monday.

2. Desk Journal (B) — the deepest long-term value: it turns the Terminal from a news tool into the firm's institutional memory, and it feeds richer content into A.

C and D slot in behind, or jump the queue if a specific need bites. All four run on the infrastructure already in place — no new subscriptions, no servers, no cost.
