SHEERSTOCK PARK · The Multi-Asset Programme
From oil desk to multi-asset firm — the infrastructure for expanding into indices, bonds & FX

The News Terminal was step one of a bigger picture: Sheerstock Park trading beyond oil. A desk can't trade a new market until it can watch it, understand what moves it, and prove it has an edge — before risking capital. This programme builds those three layers, in order. Each is independently useful; together they de-risk the whole expansion.

Phase 1 — The Multi-Asset Cockpit the watching layer

The Terminal's DNA, pointed at every market

Extend the proven machinery — live prices, curated news, AI reads, alerts — to the expansion targets:

  • Rates & bonds: the yield curve live (2y/10y/30y), bond futures, curve shape and its changes — the market that drives all others.
  • Indices & vol: S&P, Nasdaq, FTSE, DAX futures, VIX — with the big-mover detection already built.
  • FX & gold: dollar regime, majors, havens.
  • A daily AI regime read: risk-on or off, curve steepening or flattening, dollar direction — one paragraph, every morning, folded into the existing briefings.

Builds directly on the live Terminal · ~1 week of evenings · £0/month

Phase 2 — The Event Reaction Engine the understanding layer

In rates and indices, the calendar is the market

CPI, jobs numbers, Fed decisions, bond auctions — these scheduled moments are where indices and bonds actually move. This system makes the desk fluent in them:

  • Before each event: a primer email — what's expected, what's priced, and how markets reacted the last several times this number surprised.
  • A reaction memory that compounds: "the S&P has averaged a ±1% move on CPI days; 10-year yields ±8bp" — built automatically from every release the system lives through.
  • After each event: the scorecard — consensus vs actual vs market reaction, archived and searchable.

This is how a new desk builds instinct in months instead of years · ~1–2 weeks of evenings · £0/month

▼

Phase 3 — The Paper Desk the proving layer · the flagship

Prove the edge before funding it partner-grade evidence

A simulated trading book for the new markets. Logging a hypothetical position takes 30 seconds — instrument, direction, size, thesis. The system does the rest:

  • Tracks every paper trade against real prices — live P&L, win rate, drawdown, per asset class and per person.
  • Snapshots the context at entry (the Terminal's headlines, sentiment and prices at that moment) — so every trade can be reviewed against what was knowable.
  • Weekly AI review: which theses worked, which markets suit the desk's style, where the edge actually is.
  • The output after a quarter: an evidence file — "our paper record in indices vs bonds vs FX" — on which the real-capital decision can be made with data rather than instinct.

No capital at risk, no regulatory exposure — pure decision-support · ~1–2 weeks of evenings · £0/month

Suggested path

Build Phase 1 now (fastest — the Terminal already does most of it), start Phase 3 in parallel so the paper track record starts accruing immediately — its value compounds with every week it runs. Phase 2 follows and plugs into both.

Everything runs on the infrastructure already in place: no new subscriptions, no servers, no running cost. The programme's output is a desk that arrives at its first real multi-asset trade already fluent — with the evidence to size it.
