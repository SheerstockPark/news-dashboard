SHEERSTOCK PARK · What We Build Next
Proposed new internal systems — following the invoice-reconciliation dashboard and the News Terminal

Two systems are now live: the invoice-reconciliation dashboard (back office) and the News Terminal (market intelligence — dashboard, twice-daily briefings, weekly desk review, instant alerts). The same approach — AI + automation, built in-house, essentially zero running cost — can be pointed at the next problem. Four candidates:

Option A — Contract Intelligence Vault

Every agreement, searchable and self-monitoring partner-level

Drop contracts, confirmations and agreements into one private vault. AI reads each one and extracts what matters — parties, dates, quantities, payment terms, notice periods, renewal deadlines — into a live register.

  • Ask it questions in plain English: "what payment terms did we agree with X?" · "which agreements renew this quarter?" — answered with the clause quoted.
  • Never miss a date: automatic reminders ahead of expiries, renewals and notice windows.
  • One source of truth instead of folders, inboxes and memory.

Confidential by design (private, access-controlled) · ~1–2 weeks of evenings · needs: the documents themselves

Option B — Cash-Flow & Treasury Cockpit

The money picture, live sequel to invoice recon

Builds directly on the invoice-reconciliation work: one screen for cash in and out over time — expected receipts vs actual, aging receivables with automatic chase reminders, payables coming due, currency exposure at a glance, and a simple "cash runway" view for partner decisions.

Extends a system already in use · ~1 week of evenings · needs: bank/ledger exports (CSV is enough)

Option C — Compliance & KYC Tracker

Counterparty paperwork that chases itself quiet win

Every counterparty's onboarding and KYC documents in one register — what's held, what's missing, what expires when. Automatic expiry chasing, an instant "are we current with X?" answer, and a clean audit trail. Unglamorous, but it removes a recurring scramble and looks excellent in any review.

Deterministic and low-risk · ~3–4 evenings · needs: the current KYC folder/spreadsheet

Option D — Relationship Manager + Meeting Minutes AI

The firm's memory of who, what and when built for the partner seat

A lightweight relationship tracker for counterparties, brokers and contacts — last contact, open threads, history — plus an AI that turns meeting notes (or recordings) into clean minutes with action items, and chases those actions weekly. Small firms run on relationships; this makes sure nothing said in a meeting evaporates.

~1 week of evenings · needs: buy-in to drop notes/recordings into one place

How to choose — and a suggestion

The real selector is where the pain is sharpest and what data is easiest to hand over — these run on internal documents rather than public feeds, so access decides speed. On value-for-effort: A (Contract Vault) is the flagship — highest partner-level impact and the strongest showcase of what AI can do for the firm. B (Cash Cockpit) is the safest bet — it extends a tool already proving itself. A pragmatic path: B first, A second, with C as a quick win slotted in between.

Smaller add-ons to the News Terminal (a Monday partner's brief, a trade journal) are also on the shelf — happy to walk through those separately.
